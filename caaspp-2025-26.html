2025–26 test scores: what changed from last year

District results held steady after several years of decline: ELA was flat and Math rose about a point. Underneath, both disadvantaged and non-disadvantaged students held or improved, but a growing share of disadvantaged students kept the district average from moving.

How to read this page: every figure is the percentage of students who met or exceeded the standard on California's CAASPP test (called "Proficient and above" in CDE's 2026 files, the same measure under a new name). Changes are in percentage points, comparing 2025–26 with 2024–25. This compares two years of one test, so a single year's change isn't a confirmed trend, especially for small schools and small student groups.

District overall

ELA stopped declining and Math ticked up for the first time since 2021–22.

  • ELA was flat. Each of the four achievement levels moved by less than half a point.
  • Math improved slightly at both ends. Fewer students scored at the lowest level (32.7% → 31.3%) and more at the top (32.6% → 33.6%).

More disadvantaged students, but both groups held or improved

The share of tested students counted as socioeconomically disadvantaged rose from 40% to 45%. Within each group, results held or went up.

Why the district average barely moved: more students are now in the lower-scoring group, which pulls the average down even when both groups improve. With 2024–25's student mix held constant, 2025–26 would have been about 55.1% in ELA (vs 52.7% actual) and 53.1% in Math (vs 50.5% actual).

Sources & notes

Data: California Department of Education CAASPP research files for San Mateo-Foster City School District, 2024–25 and 2025–26 (Smarter Balanced, grades 3–8). Figures are rounded to one decimal place.

Starting in 2025–26, CDE labels the four achievement levels Advanced / Proficient / Developing / Minimal instead of Standard Exceeded / Met / Nearly Met / Not Met. This page treats them as the same four levels.

CDE hides results for groups with 10 or fewer students with scores. "Disadvantaged" means CDE's "socioeconomically disadvantaged" student group. Its share of tested students jumped from about 31% to 42% in 2023–24, which may reflect a change in how students are classified rather than a change in who attends; the data alone can't say which.
