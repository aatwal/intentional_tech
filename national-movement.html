What sparked this

One resolution, then dozens of communities followed

In April 2026, Los Angeles Unified School District — the nation's second-largest district — passed a landmark, unanimous resolution on classroom screen time, sparked by advocacy from Schools Beyond Screens. Since then, parent groups in dozens of other communities have launched their own local petitions asking their own school boards to do the same. The shared ask across nearly all of them: Kids Come First.

Source: Four Norms, National EdTech Collective Action Tracker (fournorms.com/edtech-tracker), snapshot as of mid-September 2026. Figures update live as campaigns launch and collect signatures.

Where campaigns are active

90 communities across 26 states have opened a campaign, from major metro districts to small towns. California has by far the largest concentration, with 30+ active campaigns.
