    About these numbers: CDE's statewide research files, All Students only. Each district is scored on grades 3–8 combined: the grade-level results weighted by students with scores, leaving out grades CDE suppressed (10 or fewer students). That keeps high-school and unified districts comparable to %%SHORT%%, which is K–8. County offices of education and State Board of Education-authorized charters (which CDE lists as their own one-school "districts") are listed but not ranked. 2014–15 and 2015–16 figures are built from whole-number percentages, so they can differ from CDE's own totals by up to half a point. Starting in 2025–26 CDE calls this measure "Proficient and above".
