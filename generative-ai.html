Generative AI can bypass the very practice that builds learning.

AI tools can answer instantly, but the struggle of retrieving, reasoning, and correcting mistakes is what actually builds durable learning. When students outsource that struggle to a chatbot, the practice that makes learning stick may never happen.

The nation's two largest school districts have paused student access to generative AI

On September 2, 2026, LAUSD and NYC announced pauses on student access to generative AI. Both are using this time to build safeguards for introducing AI safely and effectively.
